# Slavers enslave their own

A Slave Traders guard who is knocked out and put in a cage of his own faction's slave shop is made a slave by his own faction. He stays a member of the Slave Traders.

## Files

| File | What it is |
|---|---|
| `Slaver Cage Test.zip` | Mod. Start "Slaver Cage Test": one skeleton with every skill at 100, dressed for stealth, in Sho-Battai's market near the slave shop. |
| `SlaverCageStart.zip` | Save made right after that start, with the clock edited to 10 PM so the test runs at night. |
| `GuardInCage.zip` | A Slaver Guard from the shop, knocked out and put in one of the shop's cages. He is caged and not yet a slave. |
| `GuardEnslaved.zip` | Later in the same game: the caged Slaver Guard is now a slave, still of the Slave Traders. |

## Steps

1. Load `SlaverCageStart`.
2. Knock out one of the slave shop's Slaver Guards unseen (an assassination from stealth works), carry him into an empty cage in the shop and close it.
3. Back off out of sight and let time pass. The shop's slavers come and process him, and he is marked as a slave, though he belongs to their own faction.
