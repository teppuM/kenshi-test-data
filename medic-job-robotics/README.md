# Medic job on a skeleton

A skeleton with the Medic job but not the Robotics job does not repair itself, even while carrying Skeleton Repair Kits. Given the Robotics job, it starts repairing itself.

## Files

| File | What it is |
|---|---|
| `Medic Job Test.zip` | Mod. Start "Medic Job Test": one skeleton in Black Desert City with 10,000 cats and two Skeleton Repair Kits. |
| `MedicTest-original.zip` | Save made right after that start. |
| `MedicTest.zip` | The same save with the skeleton's arms and legs damaged to 40 of 200, the two kits in its main inventory. |

## Steps

1. Load `MedicTest`.
2. Give the skeleton only the Medic job (the MEDIC button) and unpause. It does not repair its limbs.
3. Add the Robotics job. It starts repairing itself with the kits.
